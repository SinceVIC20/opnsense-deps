This release comes from the temporary, unofficial Mono Gateway update server at mono-opnsense.sincevic20.com, not from OPNsense or Mono. It keeps PPPoE connections in hardware after a reconnect.

- offload: PPPoE connections stay in hardware after the provider reconnects. Before, a reconnect left almost all WAN traffic on the CPU until the router restarted, and setting up the connection again could crash the router.
